Frequently asked.

What services does TENTIM offer?

We specialize in luxury brand identity design, premium website development, UI/UX design, motion graphics, and digital marketing assets. Our team handles everything from concept to deployment, ensuring a seamless experience.

What information do I need to provide?

To get the best results, I will need your Business Name, Tagline (if any), a brief description of your Industry/Audience, and any color preferences or style examples (images/links) that you like.

Can you build the backend too?

Yes — logins, admin dashboards, payments, and newsletters are all part of the product-design tier.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A brand identity project typically takes 1-3 days, while a full website development may take 4-7 days. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

Do you work with a fixed price or hourly?

Fixed price per phase, agreed before work starts. Retainers are billed monthly.

What is your pricing structure?

We offer project-based pricing tailored to your specific needs. Contact us for a detailed quote. We also offer retainer packages for ongoing design and development support, perfect for growing businesses.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Absolutely! We offer comprehensive maintenance packages including regular updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, and content updates. Our support team is available via WhatsApp and email.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Yes, we excel at website redesigns. We'll conduct a thorough audit of your current site, identify improvement opportunities, and create a modern, responsive design that better serves your business goals and user needs.

What if I do not like the initial concepts?

Customer satisfaction is my priority. We will use the Revision process to tweak the colors, fonts, or layout based on your feedback. My goal is to ensure the final result is exactly what you envisioned.
